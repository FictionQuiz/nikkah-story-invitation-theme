      <h1 id="nikkah-story-invitation">Nikkah Story Invitation</h1>

<p>HOORIQA STUDIO — VIP CINEMATIC NIKKAH INVITATION</p>

<p>Build a complete, premium, fully functional, cinematic digital Nikkah invitation website for HOORIQA STUDIO.</p>

<p>This is a complete website project, not a landing page mockup, not a static design and not a collection of screenshots.</p>

<p>Build the actual working experience with functional interactions, animations, navigation, forms and responsive layouts.</p>

<ol>
  <li>BRAND IDENTITY</li>
</ol>

<p>Brand: HOORIQA STUDIO
Tagline: Every Invitation Tells a Story.</p>

<p>Design direction:</p>

<p>Ultra-premium VIP wedding experience.</p>

<p>Royal emerald green, champagne gold, ivory and subtle burgundy.</p>

<p>Cinematic lighting and elegant floral decoration.</p>

<p>Luxury editorial typography with beautiful calligraphy-inspired accents.</p>

<p>Sophisticated visual hierarchy.</p>

<p>Realistic-looking decorative elements.</p>

<p>Soft shadows, gold foil, glass effects and refined textures.</p>

<p>Avoid childish graphics, cheap gradients, excessive rounded cards and generic website templates.</p>

<p>The design must feel like an expensive custom digital wedding experience.</p>

<ol>
  <li>FIRST SCREEN — FLYING LANTERN CINEMATIC INTRO</li>
</ol>

<p>This is the most important feature.</p>

<p>Create a full-screen cinematic opening scene.</p>

<p>Scene:</p>

<p>Deep midnight emerald sky.</p>

<p>Tiny glowing stars.</p>

<p>Floating golden dust particles.</p>

<p>Soft atmospheric fog.</p>

<p>A beautiful, realistic 3D-style golden lantern floating in the center.</p>

<p>Warm light glowing from inside the lantern.</p>

<p>Animation sequence:</p>

<p>The lantern appears in the distance.</p>

<p>It slowly floats through the night sky.</p>

<p>The camera smoothly zooms towards the flying lantern.</p>

<p>The lantern moves closer to the screen.</p>

<p>Its golden light becomes brighter.</p>

<p>The lantern gracefully transforms into a luxurious wedding invitation card.</p>

<p>The card rotates gently towards the viewer.</p>

<p>The card opens with a realistic 3D unfolding animation.</p>

<p>Reveal the couple’s initials: A &amp; Z.</p>

<p>Show a beautiful invitation message.</p>

<p>Add a button: ENTER INVITATION.</p>

<p>When clicked, transition smoothly into the main scrolling wedding website.</p>

<p>Important:</p>

<p>The lantern must actually animate.</p>

<p>The camera zoom must be visible.</p>

<p>The card transformation must be animated.</p>

<p>The card must actually open.</p>

<p>Do not replace this sequence with a simple fade-in.</p>

<p>Use CSS 3D transforms, Framer Motion and layered visual elements.</p>

<p>If realistic 3D assets are unavailable, create a sophisticated layered CSS/SVG lantern with convincing depth and lighting.</p>

<p>Include a small Skip Intro option.</p>

<ol>
  <li>MAIN WEBSITE — LONG SCROLLING EXPERIENCE</li>
</ol>

<p>After the intro, create a beautiful, continuous, long-scrolling invitation website.</p>

<p>Use smooth scrolling and elegant scroll-triggered animations.</p>

<p>Section 1 — Hero</p>

<p>Full-screen luxury wedding hero.</p>

<p>Large couple initials A &amp; Z.</p>

<p>Names: Ayaan &amp; Zara.</p>

<p>Nikkah announcement.</p>

<p>Beautiful background with floral decorations.</p>

<p>Floating golden particles.</p>

<p>Animated scroll indicator.</p>

<p>Section 2 — Wedding Announcement</p>

<p>Display an elegant message:</p>

<p>“With the blessings of Allah and the love of our families, we invite you to celebrate the beginning of our forever.”</p>

<p>Use beautiful typography and animated text reveals.</p>

<p>Section 3 — Meet the Couple</p>

<p>Create separate bride and groom introduction sections.</p>

<p>Bride: Zara
Groom: Ayaan</p>

<p>Use elegant portrait placeholders that can later be replaced with uploaded photographs.</p>

<p>Add gentle image zoom, reveal animations and decorative floral frames.</p>

<p>Section 4 — Family Invitation</p>

<p>Create a beautiful family invitation message.</p>

<p>Include editable bride and groom family names.</p>

<p>Use an elegant ivory and gold layout.</p>

<p>Section 5 — Nikkah Details</p>

<p>Show:</p>

<p>Nikkah date.</p>

<p>Day.</p>

<p>Time.</p>

<p>Venue.</p>

<p>Address.</p>

<p>Use a luxury invitation-card design.</p>

<p>Make all details editable from a central configuration file.</p>

<p>Section 6 — Countdown</p>

<p>Create a live countdown timer showing:</p>

<p>Days.</p>

<p>Hours.</p>

<p>Minutes.</p>

<p>Seconds.</p>

<p>Use the editable event date.</p>

<p>The countdown must update every second and show a special completed state after the event date.</p>

<p>Section 7 — Event Schedule</p>

<p>Create elegant individual event cards for:</p>

<p>Mehndi</p>

<p>Nikkah</p>

<p>Baraat</p>

<p>Walima</p>

<p>Each card should have:</p>

<p>Its own decorative image.</p>

<p>Event date and time.</p>

<p>Venue.</p>

<p>Description.</p>

<p>Animated reveal.</p>

<p>Unique visual styling.</p>

<p>Section 8 — Venue Experience</p>

<p>Create a premium venue section.</p>

<p>Include:</p>

<p>Venue photograph.</p>

<p>Venue name.</p>

<p>Full address.</p>

<p>Get Directions button.</p>

<p>The Google Maps button must open the correct editable map URL.</p>

<p>Use a clearly marked sample map URL until the actual venue is provided.</p>

<p>Section 9 — Wedding Gallery</p>

<p>Create an elegant photo gallery.</p>

<p>Features:</p>

<p>Responsive image grid.</p>

<p>Click-to-open lightbox.</p>

<p>Next and previous image navigation.</p>

<p>Close button.</p>

<p>Smooth image transitions.</p>

<p>Use replaceable sample images.</p>

<p>Section 10 — Guest RSVP</p>

<p>Create a working RSVP form.</p>

<p>Fields:</p>

<p>Guest name.</p>

<p>Attendance confirmation.</p>

<p>Number of guests.</p>

<p>Optional message.</p>

<p>Requirements:</p>

<p>Validate required fields.</p>

<p>Display helpful error messages.</p>

<p>Show confirmation after successful submission.</p>

<p>Prevent accidental duplicate submissions.</p>

<p>Save demo submissions in localStorage.</p>

<p>Keep data handling clearly identified as local demo storage, not a real online database.</p>

<p>Section 11 — Guest Wishes</p>

<p>Create a beautiful wishes and greetings section.</p>

<p>Guests can enter:</p>

<p>Their name.</p>

<p>Their wishes.</p>

<p>Save demo wishes locally and display submitted wishes on the page.</p>

<p>Clearly explain that wishes are stored on this device only until a real database is connected.</p>

<p>Section 12 — Final Thank You</p>

<p>Create a cinematic closing section with:</p>

<p>Couple initials.</p>

<p>Elegant thank-you message.</p>

<p>Floating golden particles.</p>

<p>Floral decoration.</p>

<p>Back-to-top button.</p>

<ol>
  <li>ADVANCED ANIMATION SYSTEM</li>
</ol>

<p>Use Framer Motion where appropriate.</p>

<p>Implement:</p>

<p>Smooth scrolling.</p>

<p>Scroll-triggered reveals.</p>

<p>Parallax backgrounds.</p>

<p>Floating flower petals.</p>

<p>Golden dust particles.</p>

<p>Soft glowing light.</p>

<p>Image reveal transitions.</p>

<p>3D card tilt.</p>

<p>Elegant section transitions.</p>

<p>Animated typography.</p>

<p>Hover effects.</p>

<p>Subtle background movement.</p>

<p>Animations should feel smooth and luxurious, not excessive.</p>

<p>Respect reduced-motion accessibility settings.</p>

<ol>
  <li>FUNCTIONAL FEATURES</li>
</ol>

<p>Implement all of these:</p>

<p>Background music toggle.</p>

<p>Visible music ON/OFF status.</p>

<p>Smooth scrolling navigation.</p>

<p>Working countdown.</p>

<p>Functional RSVP form.</p>

<p>Functional guest wishes form.</p>

<p>Gallery lightbox.</p>

<p>Venue directions link.</p>

<p>Copy invitation link button.</p>

<p>Native sharing where supported, with clipboard fallback.</p>

<p>Downloadable calendar invitation (.ics).</p>

<p>Back-to-top button.</p>

<p>Mobile navigation menu.</p>

<p>Skip intro button.</p>

<p>Enter invitation button.</p>

<p>For music:</p>

<p>Do not autoplay audio without user interaction.</p>

<p>Use a replaceable audio file.</p>

<p>Provide a graceful fallback if no audio file is available.</p>

<p>For calendar:</p>

<p>Generate a valid downloadable ICS file using the editable event details.</p>

<p>For sharing:</p>

<p>Use the Web Share API when available.</p>

<p>Otherwise copy the website URL.</p>

<p>Every visible button must have a real action.</p>

<p>Never create decorative buttons that do nothing.</p>

<ol>
  <li>TECHNICAL STRUCTURE</li>
</ol>

<p>Use:</p>

<p>React.</p>

<p>TypeScript.</p>

<p>Tailwind CSS.</p>

<p>Framer Motion.</p>

<p>Lucide icons.</p>

<p>Organize the project into reusable components.</p>

<p>Suggested components:</p>

<p>LanternIntro</p>

<p>InvitationCard</p>

<p>HeroSection</p>

<p>CoupleSection</p>

<p>FamilySection</p>

<p>NikkahDetails</p>

<p>Countdown</p>

<p>EventSchedule</p>

<p>VenueSection</p>

<p>WeddingGallery</p>

<p>RSVPForm</p>

<p>GuestWishes</p>

<p>MusicController</p>

<p>Footer</p>

<p>Create a central editable wedding configuration file containing:</p>

<p>Couple names.</p>

<p>Initials.</p>

<p>Date.</p>

<p>Time.</p>

<p>Venue.</p>

<p>Address.</p>

<p>Maps URL.</p>

<p>Family names.</p>

<p>Event details.</p>

<p>Gallery images.</p>

<p>Music file path.</p>

<p>Do not hardcode wedding details in multiple components.</p>

<ol>
  <li>MOBILE AND PERFORMANCE</li>
</ol>

<p>The website must work properly on:</p>

<p>Mobile phones.</p>

<p>Tablets.</p>

<p>Desktop computers.</p>

<p>Requirements:</p>

<p>Responsive layouts.</p>

<p>Touch-friendly controls.</p>

<p>Optimized images.</p>

<p>Smooth animations.</p>

<p>No horizontal overflow.</p>

<p>No broken images.</p>

<p>No console errors.</p>

<p>Accessible labels and keyboard navigation.</p>

<p>Reduced-motion support.</p>

<ol>
  <li>DEMO CONTENT</li>
</ol>

<p>Use these editable sample details:</p>

<p>Bride: Zara
Groom: Ayaan
Initials: A &amp; Z
Nikkah date: 12 December 2027
Venue: Royal Wedding Venue (sample)
Location: Karachi, Pakistan</p>

<p>Clearly mark sample details so they can be replaced later.</p>

<ol>
  <li>FINAL QUALITY REQUIREMENTS</li>
</ol>

<p>Before completing the project:</p>

<p>Test the entire lantern animation.</p>

<p>Test the card-opening interaction.</p>

<p>Test the transition into the scrolling website.</p>

<p>Test every navigation link.</p>

<p>Test all buttons.</p>

<p>Test countdown accuracy.</p>

<p>Test RSVP validation.</p>

<p>Test guest wishes.</p>

<p>Test image gallery.</p>

<p>Test mobile responsiveness.</p>

<p>Fix broken layouts and runtime errors.</p>

<p>Do not stop after creating the visual design.</p>

<p>Build the complete working website.</p>

<p>FINAL CREATIVE DIRECTION:</p>

<p>The guest should feel that they are entering a magical royal wedding experience. The flying lantern should be the memorable signature opening of HOORIQA STUDIO. After the cinematic opening, the invitation should unfold into a beautiful, premium, interactive scrolling Nikkah website.</p>

<p>Make it visually exceptional, technically functional and original.</p>
